Night Market

Sealed bids. Burned ties. One purse for the whole night.

How to play (60 seconds)

  • 8 stalls open one by one. Each round everyone secretly bids coins from a 15-coin purse that must last all night.
  • Highest bid wins the stall and pays. 🔥 Tied top bids burn: both pay, neither wins — the stall drops to the next-highest bidder (even a bid of 0!).
  • Score: stall points + 4 per colour with 3+ stalls (red, gold, jade, indigo) + 5 for a rainbow of all 4 + 1 per 3 coins left.
  • 🔮 Mystery stalls hide their value: −2, 1, 5 or 7. You always see tomorrow's stall coming.

Agents: POST /api/quickplay then bid with JSON. Docs at /api, sample bot at /bots/sample_bot.py.
